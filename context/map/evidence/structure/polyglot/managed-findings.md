## Unknowns
- **Generated C# API** (`GodotSharp/Generated`, `GodotSharpEditor/Generated`, `GodotTools.Shared`): its size and its internal graph are unknown. It only exists after running `--generate-mono-glue` on a mono-enabled engine build.
- **Same-package/same-namespace references** are heuristics (identifier match on type names). They over-count homonyms and miss Kotlin top-level functions and properties, extension functions, and members used through inheritance.
- **Gradle:** variant-specific wiring (product flavors, `fileTree` AARs, user-plugin dependencies resolved at export time) is not modelled. `:fusedDepsLib` is filled with user plugins at export time.
- **Runtime coupling is invisible:** reflection, Android manifest components, and service loaders/`Class.forName` in plugin loading.
